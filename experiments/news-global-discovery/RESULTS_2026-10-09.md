# Tavily worldwide discovery benchmark — 2026-10-09

This experiment was isolated from the Doomscroller News pipeline. It did not
modify the dataset, application, scheduled workflow, deployment, or Beta.

## Runs

| Run | Configuration | Queries | Credits | Raw results | Unique results |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Historical baseline | verbose editorial prompts, general web index, 2026-09-29 through 2026-10-04 | 8 | 8 | 62 | 61 |
| Fresh baseline | verbose editorial prompts, general web index, previous three days | 8 | 8 | 68 | 65 |
| Corrected diagnostic | short search phrases, news index, previous three days | 8 | 8 | 41 | 41 |

Total used: 24 free credits.

## Finding

The two baseline runs failed editorially. Results were dominated by generic
homepages, US municipal documents, calendars, PDFs, institutional pages, and
other material that was not a distinct international story.

The corrected news-index run was meaningfully better. Potentially useful
candidates included reporting about craft livelihoods in Vietnam, a Nigerian
Quran-teaching tradition, Australian forest restoration, Indonesian orangutan
habitat, and cross-border restoration in the Paraná basin. However, the same
run still returned tourism pages, publicity, PDFs, generic sites, weak sources,
and a large US bias.

Tavily therefore passes as a **supplementary candidate scout**, but fails as a
standalone replacement for the editorial research that produced the user's
strong reference editions. Its results require deterministic filtering,
source-quality checks, event verification, and another discovery source.

## Cost implication

The current eight-query run costs eight credits. Twice daily for a 30-day month
would cost approximately 480 of the recurring 1,000-credit free allowance.
This leaves about 520 credits, but the limitation is discovery quality rather
than quota.

## Recommendation

Do not connect Tavily directly to production. Retain it as one experimental
input and compare the same task with another genuinely recurring, no-card
provider before designing a combined discovery architecture.
