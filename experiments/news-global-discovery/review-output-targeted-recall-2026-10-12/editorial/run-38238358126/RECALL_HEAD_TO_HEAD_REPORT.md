## What this changes

The verified edition should not be treated as finished after its first strict pass. A bounded, lane-targeted recall pass is justified because it recovered five strong, distinct stories that the broad discovery stage missed. The sustainable architecture is now:

1. broad discovery and qualification;
2. strict source verification;
3. gap audit by lane and priority region;
4. targeted recall only for demonstrated gaps;
5. source repair;
6. blind head-to-head editorial comparison;
7. event/update deduplication;
8. writer and final card checks.

This experiment does **not** justify admitting every recovered candidate or allowing recall to inflate the feed automatically. It justifies giving verified recovered stories a fair chance to replace weaker material.
