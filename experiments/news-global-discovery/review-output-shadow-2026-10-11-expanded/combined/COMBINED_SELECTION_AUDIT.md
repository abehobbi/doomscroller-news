# Combined qualified-pool selection audit

This is a shadow diagnostic only. It did not alter the app, main branch, Beta, deployment, or production data. No summary-generation request was made.

## Result

- Broad and targeted evidence pages merged: 206
- Distinct event clusters after URL and event deduplication: 144
- Selected for the qualified 65-story pool: 65
- Selected mix: 14 world major, 12 priority-region major, 19 world interesting, 14 priority-region interesting, and 6 Discovery
- The Panama earthquake's differently worded reports now form one event, not two cards.
- A false-positive Bangladesh municipal-project article returned by the Pirojpur search remains separate from the actual Pirojpur investigation.

## Targeted recoveries that won places

1. Ghana teachers suspend their two-week strike — priority major; 3 independent source families.
2. Sudan drone strike reportedly kills 41 near Ethiopia — world major; 3 source families.
3. Panama earthquake — world major; 3 source families.
4. Navi Pillay wins the Nobel Peace Prize — world major; 4 source families.
5. Canada unexpectedly loses jobs — priority major; Statistics Canada plus 3 other source families.
6. Bangladesh hospitals face dengue pressure — priority major; 2 source families.

These were admitted by the ordinary scoring and lane constraints. They were not manually appended after selection.

## Strong recoveries retained as reserves

- The Daily Star's Pirojpur public-works investigation remains qualified but outside the 65-story cut. It is a single-publisher investigation and any eventual card must preserve attribution.
- Ghana's proposed mining-law revision remains qualified but outside the cut. Its available copies trace to one Reuters reporting family and the proposal remains prospective.

## Baseline selections displaced

The six recovered events above replaced these lower-ranked baseline candidates:

- South Sudan peace-agreement pressure before elections
- A generic Canada Gazette orders-in-council page
- Syrian control of al-Shaddadi base
- Closure of the IAEA's Syria investigation
- Ghana textile-waste import campaign
- An obituary for Utah advocate Pamela Atkinson

This is an editorial improvement: concrete mass-casualty, disaster, labour, economic, health, and internationally consequential developments displaced less urgent, less specific, or weaker-source candidates.

## Remaining cautions

- Source-page headlines in this audit are evidence labels, not finished Doomscroller headlines. The Canada and Nobel entries especially need factual user-facing headlines during the writing stage.
- Selection into the 65-story pool does not guarantee inclusion in a smaller displayed edition. Final front-page ordering and card generation remain separate guarded stages.
- The reserve status of Pirojpur and Ghana mining is a real outcome of the current scoring and capacity rules, not a retrieval failure.

## Recommended next step

Run the final front-page selection against this corrected 65-story pool, preserving lane variety while allowing urgency and consequence to dominate the top positions. Only that smaller chosen set should proceed to evidence-packet preparation and Cloudflare writing.
