# Targeted major-news recovery audit

This is a shadow diagnostic only. It did not alter the app, main branch, Beta, deployment, or production data.

## Run

- Fixed comparison window: 2026-10-08 12:59 a.m. through 2026-10-11 12:59 a.m. Toronto time
- Recovery targets: 8
- Exa searches: 8
- Returned Exa cost: $0.056
- Search results: 80
- Public pages inspected: 40

- Evidence-topic mismatch pages rejected: including the generic Mission Offline page previously mistaken for the Sudan article

## Recovery result

All eight missing or evidence-deficient subjects were recovered in usable form:

1. Sudan drone strike: usable BBC evidence plus an AP report available through WTOP; the allegation remains attributed to Emergency Lawyers, with the UN reaction and lack of an RSF response preserved.
2. Panama earthquake: AFP and additional established reporting recovered.
3. Canada employment loss: Statistics Canada's primary release plus CBC, Canadian Press and economic reporting recovered.
4. Navi Pillay Nobel Peace Prize: Nobel Committee primary material plus BBC, UN News and NPR recovered.
5. Pirojpur public-works investigation: the original Daily Star investigation recovered. It remains an attributed single-publisher investigation, not independently proven wrongdoing.
6. Bangladesh dengue hospital pressure: detailed hospital reporting plus current DGHS figures reported by The Business Standard recovered.
7. Ghana mining-law revision: the Reuters report was recovered through several syndication partners. These copies count as one source family; the proposal must remain attributed and prospective.
8. Ghana teachers' strike: Graphic, Ghana News Agency, ChannelOne and Adom reporting recovered, including the distinction between strike suspension and complete resolution of the dispute.

## Safety fix prompted by this run

The evidence gate previously judged prose cleanliness and length but did not require the extracted page to discuss the discovered story. It now requires meaningful topic overlap between the search result and extracted article. This prevents generic publisher homepages or platform descriptions from masquerading as event evidence.

## Readiness

The recovered candidates are ready to be merged into the shadow candidate pool for editorial comparison. They have not been written into cards. Ghana's mining-law story and the Pirojpur investigation require explicit attribution in any eventual headline and summary because their strongest claims rest on one original reporting family.
